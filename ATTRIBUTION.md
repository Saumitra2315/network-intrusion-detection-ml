UNSW-NB15 was created by Nour Moustafa and Jill Slay and their collaborators at UNSW.
The official page grants free academic research use and asks commercial users to
agree terms with the authors. This application is an educational benchmark demo.
Raw training/test CSVs are not included in the distributable application.

[Official dataset, terms, download link, and citations](https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/unsw-nb15-dataset)

The official download redirected to Microsoft sign-in during setup. The training
and testing CSVs used here came from the public Nir-J/ML-Projects mirror; exact URLs,
SHA-256 hashes, actual row counts, and schema are recorded in artifacts/metadata.json.
Equality with the inaccessible official download is unverified.
